Non-negotiable

Human stays sovereign on two things

Everything else can be drafted, checked, and red-teamed by the agent. These two cannot be delegated away.

💡 IDEA

What is worth asking, what it means, and what is ethical. Discovery forges candidates; the human locks the question.

📊 DATA

Real, IRB-approved, never machine-originated. Raw clinical data stays local and gitignored. The skill never invents numbers.

Why this gets starred

Concrete differentiators — not vibes

Discovery before drafting

Phase −1 forges and ranks questions, checks novelty against live literature, and locks a plan — only after the human picks the IDEA.

Stage-gates that auto-fix

Eight quality gates. FAIL → structured feedback → fixer agent → re-check (up to 3 loops). No silent proceed.

Adversarial review

Red-teams the central claim. A KILL path returns to Discovery instead of polishing a bad paper.

EN + JP humanizer

18 English and 13 Japanese academic AI patterns with before/after examples and section priorities.

Six types, 20+ guidelines

Original, Case Report, Review, SR, Letter, Protocol — CARE / CONSORT / STROBE / PRISMA and more wired in.

Team mode + tested scripts

Seven parallel agents for search/draft/humanize/review. Utilities like table1.py and SR helpers with CI tests.
